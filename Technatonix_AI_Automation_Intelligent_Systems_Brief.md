## 2.1 Primary profile and portfolio

- Personal portfolio: https://www.sobansiraj.dev/
- Upwork profile: https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/muhammadsobans

His personal portfolio headline is:

> “I build automations that survive production.”

This is effective because it sells a business outcome and engineering standard rather than merely listing tools.

His positioning focuses on:
- RPA and rule-based automation.
- n8n, Make and Zapier workflows.
- AI-driven workflows and LLM integrations.
- AI voice agents and call automation.
- API and webhook integrations.
- Connecting CRMs, databases, payment systems and internal tools.
- Production reliability, error handling, monitoring and maintainability.

The strongest differentiator in his message is the emphasis on the difficult “middle layer” between business systems: reliability, idempotency, retries, error recovery and operational monitoring.

### What The Technatonix should learn

1. Sell the operational outcome, not just n8n/Make/Zapier implementation.
2. Demonstrate actual workflows and system integrations.
3. Explain how the automation behaves when APIs fail, workflows retry or data is duplicated.
4. Present AI voice agents as a concrete use case, not a vague AI promise.
5. Show proof through screenshots, workflow diagrams, demos, case studies and client outcomes.
6. Make it clear that the company can build custom backends and integrations where no-code tools are insufficient.

### What not to copy

- Do not copy his exact headline, wording, portfolio descriptions or visual identity.
- Do not position The Technatonix as an n8n-only or no-code-only agency.
- Do not claim the same projects, client outcomes, work history, ratings or earnings.
- Do not claim that every workflow is fully autonomous or can run without human oversight.
- Do not advertise tools or platforms as established delivery capabilities until the team has validated them.

# 6. The engineering differentiator: automation that is dependable

This section is important. Do not market automation as a collection of fragile demos.

Add a small “Engineered for real operations” strip or section explaining that projects are designed with:

- **Reliability:** retries, timeouts, failure paths and recovery procedures.
- **Idempotency:** safeguards against duplicate processing when events are retried.
- **Observability:** execution logs, alerts and operational visibility.
- **Security:** secret management, least-privilege access and appropriate data handling.
- **Human oversight:** approval steps for sensitive, financial or irreversible actions.
- **Maintainability:** readable workflows, documentation and handover.
- **Scalability:** queues, concurrency controls and appropriate architecture when volume grows.

Only claim a control on a project when it is actually implemented. These are engineering principles and delivery options, not blanket certifications.

Suggested micro-headline:

> **Automation should not just work in a demo. It should be dependable in day-to-day operations.**

# 12. FAQs for the landing page

### What is AI automation?

AI automation combines artificial intelligence, software integrations and workflow orchestration to help businesses automate tasks, process information and coordinate operations with appropriate human oversight.

### Can you automate our existing business software?

Yes, where suitable APIs, webhooks, connectors or other supported integration methods are available. We first assess the systems, permissions and process requirements.

### Do you only use n8n, Make and Zapier?

No. We select the right approach for each project. This may include workflow platforms, custom backend services, AI APIs and direct integrations.

### Can you build custom AI agents?

We can design task-specific AI agents that use approved tools and business data, with defined permissions, safeguards and escalation to a human when required.

### Can you build AI voice agents?

We can assess and implement voice systems for use cases such as enquiry handling, appointment scheduling and call routing, subject to the relevant platform, consent and regulatory requirements.

### Will automation replace our employees?

Our objective is to reduce repetitive work and support employees, not to remove human judgment from sensitive decisions. Approval and escalation steps can be included.

### Can you integrate AI automation with our ERP or CRM?

Yes, subject to the capabilities and integration options of the existing platform. We can assess APIs, data structures and security requirements before proposing an implementation.

### Do you provide ongoing support?

Support and monitoring can be scoped as part of the engagement, including maintenance, workflow updates and improvements.
