## 01. AI My Advance

- **Company:** Simple Code AI, an LLC under Better Accounting Solutions
- **Industry:** FinTech and merchant funding
- **Website:** [aimyadvance.com](https://www.aimyadvance.com/)

AI My Advance is a financial operations platform for the merchant cash advance
industry. Its positioning brings together funding deals, payments and financial
administration for funders, syndicators, sales organizations and merchants.

**Key capabilities:**

- Funding deal management and underwriting workflows
- Payment and collection tracking
- Syndication and financial reconciliation

## 02. EquipMate

- **Company:** [Nextek Systems](https://www.nexteksystems.com/)
- **Industry:** Equipment maintenance and asset management
- **Website:** [getequipmate.com](https://getequipmate.com/)

EquipMate is a computerized maintenance management system (CMMS) designed to
help teams manage equipment and maintenance activities. Its product positioning
focuses on organized maintenance workflows and better visibility into assets.

**Key capabilities:**

- Preventive maintenance scheduling
- Work-order management
- Maintenance inventory and asset tracking

## 03. SynapticFlow

- **Company:** CyberSoft North America
- **Industry:** Strategic planning and AI-assisted operations
- **Website:** [synapticflow.ai](https://synapticflow.ai/)

SynapticFlow is a strategic and operational planning product. Its Hoshin Kanri
approach connects long-term goals with annual objectives, operational
priorities and performance indicators, supported by AI-assisted planning.

**Key capabilities:**

- Hoshin Kanri strategy alignment
- Goals, priorities and KPI tracking
- AI-assisted planning and decision support

## 04. Qontango

- **Company:** CyberSoft North America
- **Industry:** Debt collection and financial technology
- **Website:** [qontango.com](https://qontango.com/)

Qontango is a platform for debt collection and recovery businesses. It brings
account management, collection workflows, reporting and financial records into
a centralized system.

**Key capabilities:**

- Debt collection and account management
- Customer and sales workflows
- Financial reporting and records

## 05. KREVIX ERP

- **Company:** Technatonix
- **Industry:** Manufacturing and enterprise resource planning
- **Status:** In planning, design, development and testing
- **Website:** [Technatonix products](https://www.technatonix.com/products)

KREVIX ERP is a manufacturing-focused product in development. Its
inventory-first approach is intended to connect procurement, warehouse
management, production planning and financial workflows.

**Planned capabilities:**

- Inventory-first manufacturing workflows
- Production planning and work orders
- Warehouse, procurement and finance integration

## 06. EFWRSX Cloud

- **Company:** Technatonix
- **Industry:** Field services and digital reporting
- **Status:** In planning, design, development and testing
- **Website:** [Technatonix products](https://www.technatonix.com/products)

EFWRSX Cloud, or Electronic Field Work Reporting System eXchange, is a
field-work reporting product in development. It is designed to help service
professionals capture on-site evidence, validate required information and
share job-completion reports with customers.

**Planned capabilities:**

- Mobile-first capture of photos, checklists and notes
- Required-evidence validation and job-completion reporting
- Offline drafts, branded PDFs and secure sharing

## 09. Peace Giving

- **Organization:** Mercy to Humanity
- **Industry:** Charity technology and fundraising

## Portfolio distinctions

- **Merchant funding and debt recovery:** AI My Advance focuses on merchant
  funding and syndication, whereas Qontango focuses on debt collection and
  recovery workflows.
- **Strategy and governance:** SynapticFlow emphasizes strategy alignment and
  AI-assisted planning; MetrixPlus emphasizes performance and governance;
  ORG Plus focuses on organizational structures.
- **Laundry and maintenance:** LinenTech addresses commercial laundry
  operations, LinenIQ focuses on linen tracking and distribution, and
  EquipMate focuses on equipment maintenance.
- **Services and retail:** K9 Bookkeeping is a financial services business;
  Daar Al Khuld is a fragrance and e-commerce business.
- **Products in development:** KREVIX ERP and EFWRSX Cloud are Technatonix
  products in planning, design, development and testing. Their listed
  capabilities should not be interpreted as a guarantee of availability.
